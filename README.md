## My Favorite Series: *Finding Her Edge*

**Finding Her Edge** is a Netflix figure skating drama about first love, family, and second chances.

> Adriana Russo gave up skating after losing her mom, but a champion still lives in her.

### Main Characters

1. **Adriana Russo** (Madelyn Keys): a former ice dancer who returns to the rink
2. **Freddie O'Connell** (Olly Atkins): her old partner and first love
3. **Brayden Elliot** (Cale Ambrozic): her new skating partner

### Why I Love It

- The mix of sports and romance
- The love triangle
- The story of the Russo sisters and their family rink
- The skating scenes
